What is it?

NicePortal is a Captive Portal with some interesting wikipedia articles, games, and a guestbook for you to sign.

It makes use of a small microcontroller (ESP8266 for the nerds) which hosts a local web server

Why is it called NicePortal?

Typically, these devices are used for evil portals! They disguise themselves as free wifi and phish the hell out of people

Luckily for you I'm not doing that, but please stop connecting to every sketchy wifi network you see :(
